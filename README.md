# CV-WEBSITE - Antoni Agustí Mayol de la Paz
This is a project for "Multimèdia i Interfícies de l'usuari" done it in "Universitat de Girona".

## 1. Project Analysis

### User Profile (User Persona)
The final user of this website will be the CEO or Audio/Technical Director of a video game development company. This user is looking for a versatile profile with strong musical knowledge to work as a composer and producer of video game soundtracks (BSOs), while also understanding the technical side of game creation. Furthermore, in this CV the user expects to quickly find my academic degrees (combining technological sciences and professional saxophone studies), my work experience demonstrating responsibility and English fluency, and direct visual access to my personal and university projects as a musician, designer, and programmer.

### Information Architecture

For this single-page CV website, the information architecture has been designed following a clean, top-to-bottom vertical flow that guides the user's eye naturally through my profile. Instead of overloading the screen with multiple competing columns, a single central container is used to maximize readability and visual impact. 

At the very top of the page, the header acts as an immediate visual introduction: it features a full-width stage photograph of myself playing the saxophone, accompanied by two floating contact pills in the upper corners (telephone number on the left and email address on the right) so the recruiter can contact me immediately. Right below the image, a high-contrast white banner displays my full name in large uppercase letters, serving as the structural anchor of the page.

Below the header, the main body is divided into three clearly differentiated sections separated by horizontal purple lines:
1. **Academic Information:** Placed first to immediately highlight the dual background in Technological Sciences and the Professional Music Program.
2. **Laboral Information:** Organized into distinct blocks for each company, using bulleted lists to allow fast scanning of achievements and language skills.
3. **My Projects:** Located at the bottom as a three-column visual gallery with framed thumbnails that link directly to my creative and technical portfolio.

### Visual Design
As I want a modern but clean website, the best choice for the color palette is the following one:
- **#9418B0 Royal Orchid:** The main color of the CV. It represents virtuosity and passion for music, and is used as the color of the section titles and details.
- **#000F08 Onyx:** Delicate and sophisticated. It is used as the color of the background.
- **#F4FFF8 Mint Cream:** The color of the text and details. Clean and smooth.

Regarding the typographies and layout decisions, a careful selection of Google Fonts (previously integrated in FIGMA) was integrated to establish a rigorous visual hierarchy across the single-page layout, ensuring that both aesthetic and functional goals are fully met:
- **Bebas Neue Light/Bold 113 - The Main Name:** Applied directly to the prominent full-width banner in the header to capture immediate attention and establish a strong personal brand identity.
- **Bebas Neue Regular 96 - The Section Titles:** Used for the main headings (`ACADEMIC INFORMATION`, `LABORAL INFORMATION`, and `MY PROJECTS`) to maintain design consistency and visual rhythm down the page.
- **Barlow Condensed Regular - The information:** Utilized for all body copy, including the academic list items and the laboral information entries, ensuring compact, legible, and clean text distribution.
- **Basic Regular 24 - The contact information on the top side:** Styled for the phone number and email address badges floating over the header image, providing maximum clarity and accessibility.

Overall, the single-page layout relies on balanced proportions, high-contrast elements, and dedicated spacing to deliver a professional interface tailored to the expectations of technical recruiters and industry executives.
